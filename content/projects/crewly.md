[Crewly](https://crewly.rawley.me) is an implementation of a spec sheet designed from my professor, came to life in a group of three. The idea is simple: make an app for volunteers and people looking volunteers, and here is a list of minimum features it must have. Everything else, like which technologies and how we work together to accomplish this goal, was entirely in our hands.

![Dashboard screenshot](/static/images/projects/crewly/crewly-screenshot-dashboard.png)

My role in the project was three-fold: lead the project and team members as a "scrum master", create initial code and make important design decisions early on, and contribute to the code in both the frontend and backend. And of course, document the code and the entire process. We were only given about a week to do this, and I must say I am very happy with the results and work of both myself and my team.
